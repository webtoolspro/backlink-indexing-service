QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before adding IndexPilot to your SEO workflow.

What does a backlink indexing service do?

It helps organize and process backlink URLs through an indexing workflow. Search-engine indexing itself is controlled by the search engine, so no legitimate service can guarantee a specific indexing outcome.

Can I submit URLs in bulk?

Yes. The interface is designed around bulk URL submissions and campaign organization. The production implementation can connect the form to your chosen backend or indexing provider.

Do you guarantee Google indexing?

No. We do not promise or misrepresent search-engine indexing. The platform is designed to improve your submission and monitoring workflow.

Can this become a SaaS dashboard?

Yes. Authentication, payments, database storage, API integrations, usage limits and customer dashboards can be added as the next development phase.

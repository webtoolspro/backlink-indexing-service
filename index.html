QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Important information before starting a backlink indexing campaign.

Does 100% mean guaranteed Google indexing?

No service can legitimately guarantee that a search engine will index every submitted URL. The 100% messaging refers to submission/service coverage, not a guaranteed search-engine indexing outcome.

How do I place an order?

Select a plan and use its WhatsApp button. A customized message with the selected plan details will open directly in WhatsApp.

Can I enquire for a custom requirement?

Yes. Use the Enterprise Plan's Talk to Us button to discuss your requirements directly on WhatsApp.

What are the available plans?

Starter: 20 URLs ₹400, Growth: 100 URLs ₹1,899, Mega: 500 URLs ₹8,999. Enterprise is available on a custom requirement basis.

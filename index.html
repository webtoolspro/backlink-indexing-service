Important information before starting a backlink indexing campaign.

Does 100% mean guaranteed Google indexing?

No service can legitimately guarantee that a search engine will index every submitted URL. The website therefore uses 100% to describe submission coverage and service workflow, not a guaranteed search-engine decision.

How do I place an order?

Select a plan, complete the enquiry form and send the prepared message on WhatsApp to +91 93102 71105.

Can I enquire for more than 2,000 URLs?

Yes. Send your requirements on WhatsApp and the team can discuss a custom requirement.

What are the available plans?

Demo: 5 URLs ₹100, Starter: 20 URLs ₹400, Growth: 100 URLs ₹1,899, Mega: 500 URLs ₹8,999 and Enterprise: 2,000 URLs ₹34,999.
